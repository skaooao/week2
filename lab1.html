The Little Apple’s Journey

There is an apple tree in the middle of a meadow.
The tree is full of ripe apples.

You're the biggest apple.
You've always had one dream: to be eaten.

To find someone who’s willing to eat you,
you swing your body with all your might.

And so, you fell.

You roll and roll until you roll into a little stream.

You came across a duck.

“Thank you. You’re quite a big one… But my little beak is far too small to eat you.”

“Hello, little duck. Are you hungry? Perhaps… you could have a bite of me.”

The duck swam away, and you continued on your journey.

You came across a rock.

“Hello, little rock. Are you hungry? Perhaps… you could have a bite of me.”

“I’m only a stone. I’ve never known what hunger feels like… But keep going. I’m sure you’ll meet someone who does.”

You remember what the duck said. If you were only a little smaller… perhaps the stone could help.
Then the duck might be able to eat you after all.

Ask the stone for help Keep drifting

You borrow the stone's strength and become smaller.

“Oh no... I’m only a little piece now.”

“Are you the apple I met before?”

“Yes! You’re small enough now. Thank you!”

End 1: The Duck

The duck finds you at last and carries you safely home.
